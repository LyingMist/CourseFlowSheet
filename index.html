Introduction

This flowsheet organizes courses by academic year and quarter. It is designed to make prerequisite paths, concurrent courses, and future course options easier to understand.

Color Indicators

Current course

Gold marks the course currently being explored.

←
Direct prerequisite

Light blue and a left arrow identify a first-level prerequisite.

«
Earlier prerequisite

The palest blue marks a second-level prerequisite in the course chain.

→
Unlocked course

Dark blue shows a course directly unlocked by the selected course.

↔
Concurrent course

Teal identifies a paired course that must be taken at the same time.

What Units Mean

Generally, the value assigned to a course is determined at the rate of 1 unit for every 3 hours of student work required each week during a 10-week term.

Each course shows its own units when hovered or focused. The number in brackets beside a quarter name is the sum of all course units scheduled for that quarter.
